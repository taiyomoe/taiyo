## Overview

Sheets are slide-out panels that appear from the side of the screen, providing additional content or functionality without navigating away from the current page. They're perfect for forms, settings, or detailed views that need to be easily accessible but don't require a full page transition.

<Canvas of={SheetStories.Default} />

### Non-dismissible

Non-dismissible sheets prevent users from closing the panel by clicking outside or pressing escape, requiring them to use the provided close button or action to dismiss the sheet.

<Canvas of={SheetStories.Dismissible} />

### Sides

Sheets can appear from the left, right, top, or bottom of the screen.
