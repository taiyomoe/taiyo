## Overview

Labels provide accessible text descriptions for form controls and interactive elements. They improve usability by clearly identifying what each control does and ensure proper accessibility for screen readers and other assistive technologies.

<Canvas of={LabelStories.Default} />

### Disabled

Disabled labels are associated with disabled form controls, providing visual feedback that the option is not available while maintaining proper accessibility relationships.
