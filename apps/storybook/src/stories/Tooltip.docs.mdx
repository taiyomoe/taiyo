## Overview

Tooltips reveal a short hint about a control when it's hovered, focused, or long-pressed. Wrap your tree in `TooltipProvider` once, then compose individual tooltips with `Tooltip`, `TooltipTrigger`, and `TooltipPopup`.

<Canvas of={TooltipStories.Default} />

### Sides

The `side` prop on `TooltipPopup` anchors the popup to a side of the trigger.

<Canvas of={TooltipStories.Sides} />

### Alignment

`align` controls where the popup sits along the chosen side — useful when the trigger is wider than the tooltip.

<Canvas of={TooltipStories.Alignment} />

### Default Open

`defaultOpen` shows the tooltip on mount — handy for onboarding hints or coach marks.

<Canvas of={TooltipStories.DefaultOpen} />

### Disabled

A disabled tooltip never opens, no matter the trigger interaction.

<Canvas of={TooltipStories.Disabled} />
