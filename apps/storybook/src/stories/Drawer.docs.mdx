## Overview

Drawers are swipeable, edge-anchored overlays that work especially well on touch devices. Use them for mobile-friendly forms, filters, navigation, or contextual settings that should slide in over the current view.

<Canvas of={DrawerStories.Default} />

### Positions

Drawers can be anchored to any edge of the viewport via the `position` prop. The swipe direction is inferred automatically from the position.

<Canvas of={DrawerStories.Positions} />

### Straight

The `straight` variant keeps the drawer flush with the viewport edge and drops the corner radius, making it feel like a docked panel rather than a sliding sheet.

<Canvas of={DrawerStories.Straight} />

### Inset

The `inset` variant detaches the drawer from the viewport edges with a margin and rounds every corner, mirroring the inset sheet treatment.

<Canvas of={DrawerStories.Inset} />

### With bar

Pass `showBar` to render a drag handle along the leading edge, signaling that the drawer can be dismissed via swipe.

<Canvas of={DrawerStories.WithBar} />

### With close button

By default the drawer is dismissed via swipe or backdrop click. Pass `showCloseButton` to expose an explicit close affordance in the top-right corner.

<Canvas of={DrawerStories.WithCloseButton} />
