## Overview

Toasts are short, transient messages that surface the outcome of an action — saved, failed, syncing — without interrupting the user's flow. Wrap your app in `ToastProvider` and dispatch toasts imperatively through the `Toast.useToastManager()` hook or the exported `toastManager`.

<Canvas of={ToastStories.Default} />

### Types

The `type` field on a toast applies semantic styling and renders the matching icon: `success`, `error`, `info`, `warning`, and `loading` (with an animated spinner).

### Positions

The `position` prop on `ToastProvider` pins the viewport to one of six anchor points. Vertical and horizontal swipe-to-dismiss directions are derived automatically.
