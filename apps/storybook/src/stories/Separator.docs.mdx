## Overview

Separators visually and semantically divide related groups of content, like sections of a list, navigation links, or paragraphs of text.

<Canvas of={SeparatorStories.Default} />

### Orientations

Use `orientation="horizontal"` (the default) to split stacked content and `orientation="vertical"` to divide inline elements; vertical separators stretch to the height of their container.
