## Overview

Badges are compact labels that highlight status, metadata, or counts next to other UI. They are passive by default but can render as buttons or links via the `render` prop when interactive.

<Canvas of={BadgeStories.Default} />

### Variants

Badges come in several semantic variants — a primary `default`, a muted `secondary`, an `outline`, `destructive`, and the tonal `error`/`info`/`success`/`warning` family for status surfaces.

<Canvas of={BadgeStories.Variants} />

### Sizes

Badges scale from `sm` to `lg` to fit different layout densities.
