## Overview

Accordions are vertically stacked, expandable sections used to show and hide related content. They help reduce visual noise on dense pages — like FAQs or settings panels — by letting users focus on one section at a time.

<Canvas of={AccordionStories.Default} />

### Single

By default a single panel may be open at a time. Opening another collapses the previous one.

<Canvas of={AccordionStories.Single} />

### Multiple

Pass `multiple` to allow several panels to stay open simultaneously.

<Canvas of={AccordionStories.Multiple} />

### Disabled

Pass `disabled` on the root (or an individual item) to prevent interaction.
