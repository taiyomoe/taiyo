## Overview

`Menu` is a trigger-driven dropdown built on Base UI's menu primitive. Compose a `MenuTrigger` with a `MenuPopup` and any combination of items, separators, group labels, checkbox or radio selections, and nested submenus.

<Canvas of={MenuStories.Default} />

### With Groups and Labels

Use `MenuGroup` + `MenuGroupLabel` to partition the menu into titled sections separated by `MenuSeparator`.

<Canvas of={MenuStories.WithGroupsAndLabels} />

### With Checkbox Items

`MenuCheckboxItem` toggles a boolean and renders a check indicator when active.

<Canvas of={MenuStories.WithCheckboxItems} />

### With Switch Checkbox Items

Set `variant="switch"` on `MenuCheckboxItem` to render the toggle as a switch on the trailing edge, ideal for inline settings.

<Canvas of={MenuStories.WithSwitchCheckboxItems} />

### With Radio Items

`MenuRadioGroup` + `MenuRadioItem` create a single-select group within the menu.

<Canvas of={MenuStories.WithRadioItems} />

### With Submenu

`MenuSub`, `MenuSubTrigger`, and `MenuSubPopup` nest a menu inside another, opening to the inline-end of the parent item.

<Canvas of={MenuStories.WithSubmenu} />

### With Disabled Items

Pass `disabled` to any `MenuItem` to make it unselectable while staying visible.

<Canvas of={MenuStories.WithDisabledItems} />

### Inset

The `inset` prop on `MenuItem` and `MenuGroupLabel` reserves leading space, useful for aligning text in menus that mix items with and without leading icons or indicators.

<Canvas of={MenuStories.Inset} />
