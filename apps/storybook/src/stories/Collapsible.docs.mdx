## Overview

Collapsibles let users hide and reveal a region of content under a trigger. Use them to compress secondary content, expandable settings, or progressive disclosure within a page without taking the user out of context.

<Canvas of={CollapsibleStories.Default} />

### Open by default

Pass `defaultOpen` to render the panel expanded on mount while keeping the component uncontrolled.

<Canvas of={CollapsibleStories.OpenByDefault} />

### Disabled

When `disabled` is set, the trigger no longer toggles the panel and is styled as inactive.
